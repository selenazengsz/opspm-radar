# Data and code acknowledgements

OpsPM Tracker does not claim that historical H-1B activity guarantees sponsorship for a specific opening.

- Job leads are derived from the public [Summer 2027 Role Index](https://github.com/NyXkim5/summer-2027-role-index) and [New Grad Jobs 2027](https://github.com/zapplyjobs/New-Grad-Jobs-2027). Always apply through and verify the employer's own posting.
- Company-level H-1B history is derived from the official [USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/h-1b-employer-data-hub). The compact employer index and methodology were informed by [Automated List of Summer 2027 and Fall 2026 Tech Internships](https://github.com/zshah101/Automated-List-Of-Summer-2027-and-Fall-2026-Tech-Internships), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Shah Zain.
- PMRadar is a product-design and recruiting-timeline reference only. Its branding, copy, assets, and private implementation are not included.

Historical filing or approval data is evidence of past employer behavior only. It does not establish current policy, internship eligibility, or sponsorship for any specific role.
